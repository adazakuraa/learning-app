# 接続詞

- **接続詞**は、単語・フレーズ・文をつなぐ、「and」などの言葉のことです。
    - **but**：しかし（I wanted to go, but I was too tired.）
    - **or**：または（Would you prefer tea or coffee?）
    - **so**：だから（The window was open, so the room was cold.）
    - **because**：なぜなら（I stayed home because I had a fever.）
    - **when**：〜のとき（When the alarm went off, I was still asleep.）
    - **if**：もし〜なら（If you need help, just ask.）
    - **although/though**：〜だけれども（Although it was raining, they went for a walk.）
    - **before/after**：〜の前に/後に（Stretch before you exercise.）
    - **until**：〜するまで（Wait here until I get back.）
    - **while**：〜している間（She listened to music while she was jogging.）
